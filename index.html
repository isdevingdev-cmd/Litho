Lore

The statue that woke up

Homer sent his heroes to the Elysian Fields. Litho was carved to keep them company: a small kouros with a laurel wreath, standing still for a very long time.

One night the marble split. A cool light came through the cracks, and the statue took its first step.

He has no mouth. He speaks with his eyes and his cracks. The more he cracks, the brighter he gets, until the light is all there is and the marble closes up to start again.
